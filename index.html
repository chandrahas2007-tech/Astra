The problem statement

Making orbital congestion visible

Develop a user-friendly web dashboard that collects data on real incidents and objects from publicly available satellite catalogues, analyses how long objects persist in orbit, and visualises orbital congestion. It ranks objects and orbit zones by risk, based on their orbits and trajectories, to support space research and development as well as teaching.

Active satellites can share their position and manoeuvre. Dead ones cannot, so the risk grows quietly.

Astra does not replace these systems. It uses the information they publish and adds an analysis and teaching layer.

What Astra does

One web dashboard: track, predict, act

01

Track

  • Active and inactive satellite counts
  • Last reported orbital data
  • Data freshness and objects needing a tracking update
02

Predict

  • Future orbital movement
  • Prediction uncertainty
  • Expected collision events
03

Act

  • Risk orbit zones for future launches
  • Dead satellites near re-entry
  • Re-entry timing and area alerts

Also included: a record of real incidents and their studies, for research and teaching. Every number carries its uncertainty and data age.
